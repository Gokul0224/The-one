03 / SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE

UNDERSTANDING EARTH ORBIT

LOW EARTH ORBIT

Low Earth Orbit (LEO) extends up to approximately 2,000 kilometres above Earth. Many Earth-observation satellites operate in this orbital region.

ORBITAL PERIOD

An orbital period is the time taken by a spacecraft to complete one revolution around Earth. It depends on orbital altitude.

MICROGRAVITY

Spacecraft in orbit experience continuous free fall around Earth. This creates a microgravity environment for experiments.
