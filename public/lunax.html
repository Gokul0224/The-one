02 / LUNAR SCIENCE

DISCOVER THE MOON

LUNAR SOUTH POLE

The lunar South Pole contains ancient terrain and permanently shadowed regions. Scientists study these areas to understand the Moon's history and possible resources.

WATER ICE RESEARCH

Scientists investigate whether water ice exists in permanently shadowed lunar regions and how it could support future scientific exploration.

MOON GEOLOGY

Lunar rocks and craters preserve clues about the formation of the Moon, Earth and the early solar system.

03 / NASA ARTEMIS PROGRAM

RETURNING TO THE MOON

NASA's Artemis campaign aims to expand human and robotic exploration around and on the lunar surface.

ARTEMIS

A NASA exploration campaign using human and robotic missions to advance lunar science and prepare for future Mars exploration.

ORION SPACECRAFT

Orion is designed to carry astronauts on missions beyond low Earth orbit, including journeys to the Moon.

LUNAR GATEWAY

Gateway is a planned space station around the Moon that will support science and future lunar missions.

04 / GATEWAY SCIENCE

A STATION AROUND THE MOON

UNIQUE ORBIT

NASA's Gateway is planned for a Near-Rectilinear Halo Orbit (NRHO) around the Moon.

SPACE RESEARCH

Gateway will support research in heliophysics, human health, space biology and astrophysics.

INTERNATIONAL PARTNERS

NASA is working with international partners including ESA, JAXA and CSA on the Gateway program.
