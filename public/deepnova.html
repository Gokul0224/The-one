03 / REAL NASA MISSION

VOYAGER DISCOVERY

NASA's Voyager spacecraft are among humanity's greatest deep-space exploration achievements.

VOYAGER 1

Launched on September 5, 1977. It explored Jupiter and Saturn before entering interstellar space in August 2012.

VOYAGER 2

Launched on August 20, 1977. It visited Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune before entering interstellar space in November 2018.

GOLDEN RECORD

Both Voyager spacecraft carry a golden record containing sounds and images representing life and culture on Earth.
